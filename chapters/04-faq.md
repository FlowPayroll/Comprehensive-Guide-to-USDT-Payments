# Chapter 4 — Frequently asked questions about USDT

### Is USDT equivalent to money held in a US-dollar bank account?

No. USDT is a digital token intended to maintain a value near one US dollar. It is distinct from a bank deposit, carries issuer and market risks, and is not guaranteed to trade at exactly $1 at all times.

### Which USDT network has the smallest fees?

There is no universally cheapest network at all times. Fees depend on Tron resources, BNB Smart Chain gas, Ethereum activity, and any service charges. Compare live transfer costs **and** the recipient's supported deposit networks. See **[Chapter 1](01-networks.md)**.

### Could USDT be lost when I choose the wrong chain?

Yes. A deposit to a network the destination does not support may be inaccessible. Some custodial providers can attempt recovery in limited situations, but it may be unavailable or costly. Check network compatibility before sending.

### Why do ERC20 and BEP20 addresses both start with `0x`?

Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain use similar address formats. That resemblance does not mean a recipient supports deposits on both networks. The selected blockchain remains essential.

### Are receiving USDT payments free?

A wallet typically does not charge a separate network fee simply to receive an on-chain transfer, but the sender must cover network costs. Custodial services can charge deposits, withdrawals, or other account fees. Review each provider's published schedule.

### How quickly is a payment available?

Block confirmation times depend on the chain and current conditions. Exchanges and other services may impose additional confirmations, compliance checks, or crediting delays. Do not assume that an on-chain transaction will be immediately spendable.

### Are there tax obligations when receiving USDT?

Potentially. The treatment of crypto payments differs by country and transaction type. Keep invoices, dates, amounts, fiat valuations, and transaction identifiers, and check the relevant tax requirements.

### Is a hardware wallet necessary?

Not for every use case, but it can reduce exposure of private keys for users managing meaningful self-custodied assets. Good backups, careful transaction verification, and secure account practices are important regardless of wallet type.

### What should I do before receiving a large transfer?

Confirm the exact chain and token, verify the receiving address independently, and consider a small preliminary transfer. Wait until the destination credits it correctly before proceeding with the remaining amount.

### Can FlowPayroll provide bank or USDT withdrawals?

[FlowPayroll](https://flowpayroll.net) describes withdrawals through selected banking destinations and USDT networks including TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20. Availability, review requirements, eligibility, and processing timelines are service-specific and should be checked directly; no particular completion time is guaranteed here.
